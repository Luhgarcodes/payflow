Practice project: PayFlow wallet and payments API

The full specification is in project-requirements.html. The milestones below are its big steps.

  1. Foundation: git, lint, validated config, migrations, Jest, Pino, OpenAPI and an integer-paise money utility Covers areas 2, 3, 4, 5, 9, 10 and 13
  2. JWT auth with refresh rotation, transaction PIN, KYC tiers, rate limiting and PII encryption Covers areas 6 and 7
  3. Double-entry ledger, wallets, and top-ups through a mock payment gateway with signed webhooks Covers areas 3, 5 and 8
  4. P2P transfers, merchant payment links and refunds, with idempotency keys and row locking Covers areas 3, 5, 7 and 12
  5. Statements, spending insights, fraud velocity checks and a concurrency load test Covers areas 1, 5 and 7
  6. Scheduled payments, settlements, reconciliation and outgoing merchant webhooks Covers area 8
  7. Docker, CI, cloud deployment and a design doc for scaling to 10,000 transactions per second Covers areas 11 and 12
